INDEPENDENT AUTHORITY

Trustee console

This machine holds one share of the election key. Together, trustees can unlock the totals. An individual ballot stays encrypted.

- -

Your part in the count

Before applying your share, this machine downloads the bulletin board, re-verifies every block against the validator quorum, and recomputes the encrypted totals from the ballots itself. If any of that disagrees with the ballot box, it refuses.

    What this console cannot do
    • It cannot decrypt an individual ballot - only the totals.
    • It cannot export or display your key share.
    • It cannot act on its own, or on the commission's schedule.
    • It cannot produce a result alone: a threshold of trustees is required.
