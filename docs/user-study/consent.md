# Participant information and consent

**Study:** Checking that an online voting app records your vote correctly
**Researchers:** [Student name], M.Tech CSE, SRMIST; supervisor [Supervisor name]
**Ethics approval:** [Committee name and reference number]

## What you will do

You will use a research voting app on a computer and, for some votes, on your
own phone. You will vote four times for fictional candidates named on cards we
give you, then answer short questionnaires. It takes about 30 minutes.

## What we record

How long each task takes, whether the checks worked for you, your questionnaire
answers, and notes of what you say about the app. We do not record your name
with your answers, we do not record your phone's contents, and no real election
or political opinion is involved.

## Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time without giving a reason. At
the end we will explain the study fully; you may then ask us to delete your data.

## Risks and benefits

There are no known risks beyond ordinary computer use. You will help make online
voting checks easier to use.
