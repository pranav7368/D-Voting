# Comprehension questions

Asked after both conditions, before the debrief. One point per correct answer.

1. **Who could find out which candidate you voted for?**
   (a) the registration service (b) the ballot box (c) the trustees, together, for your ballot alone (d) nobody on their own ✔
2. **What does the tracking code let you check?**
   (a) whom you voted for (b) that your ballot is in the public record ✔ (c) the current results
3. **Why can a tested ballot not be counted?**
   (a) it has a mistake in it (b) testing reveals how it was locked, so it is no longer secret ✔ (c) the app ran out of time
4. **Why does the check use your phone rather than the voting computer?**
   (a) phones are faster (b) the computer could lie about its own test ✔ (c) it does not matter
5. **What does "confirmed by independent observers" mean?**
   (a) observers saw how you voted (b) observers keep their own copy of the record and saw the same one you did ✔ (c) the election commission approved your vote
