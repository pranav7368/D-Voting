> telling you would have changed how you behaved.
>
> In some of your votes, we had made the app misbehave on purpose. It recorded a

>

> Whether or not you noticed tells us about the app's design, not about you. Many
> people do not notice in one condition or the other, which is exactly why we are
> studying it.
>
> No real vote was involved, and the candidates were made up. If you would rather
> we did not use your answers now that you know this, tell us and we will delete
> them. Do you have any questions?
